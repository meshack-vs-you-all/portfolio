About

The person
behind the code.

I'm Meshack Mogire — a full-stack engineer and founder based in Nairobi, Kenya. I've spent over five years building production digital systems for real businesses: complete web platforms, AI-powered agents, M-Pesa and WhatsApp integrations, self-hosted infrastructure, and automated content pipelines.

I founded Crafted Edge Solutions to bring the same quality of digital infrastructure that large companies use to small and growing businesses in Kenya and across Africa. I also built Wellness Solutions Africa — a full health platform that handles bookings, payments, and AI-assisted client management.

I don't hand off to someone else. I architect the system, write the code, deploy it, and keep it running. That's why my rates reflect the full value of what you get.

I work with Claude Code and local AI infrastructure daily as a tool — not for demos. I run Groq LLaMA 70B as my primary agent, GPU-accelerated Ollama inference locally, and automated pipelines that run on their own.
